Park Map Features

Art Style

Designed to look like a hand-drawn parchment map with a nautical theme, featuring a compass rose, anchors, and sea serpents in the corners.

Main Attractions
  • The Tornado Funnel & Aqua Blast Tower: Large, multi-colored thrill slides.
  • Surf Bay Wave Pool: A central hub for swimming.
  • Splash Zone: A dedicated area for kids and toddlers.
  • Lazy River Meander: A winding water path encircling the park.
Amenities

Includes labels for "Tropical Grille" (food), "Relaxation Decks," and guest services.

Legend

A "Key" in the bottom right uses icons to categorize slides, pools, food, retail, and restrooms.

Explorer's Guide

This is an "Explorer’s Guide" map for the fictional Mystik Islands Theme Park, designed with a charming, vintage parchment aesthetic. It divides the park into several distinct themed lands:

Themed Zones
Volcano Peak

Features a massive erupting volcano with the "Magma Coaster."

Starport Cosmos

A futuristic area with sci-fi attractions like the "Interstellar Blaster" and "Galaxy Starflyer."

Waterworld Lagoon

A large central aquatic area featuring a wave pool and relaxation groves.

Lost Jungle Adventure

An exotic zone containing the "Lava River Rapids" and "Treetop Trek."

Magic Kingdom

A classic fantasy section with the "Royal Castle Carousel."

Design Details
  • Visual Style: Nautical and adventurous, decorated with sea monsters, anchors, and an ornate compass rose.
  • Amenities: Includes "The Explorer’s Grille" for dining and a "Merchandise Bazaar" for shopping.
  • Interactive Legend: The "Key" at the bottom right uses thematic terms like Aura Spheres and Silent Sanctuary to guide guests.
